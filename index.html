Flagship AI Product

01 / TradeLens — Multimodal AI Trading Assistant

A decision-support platform designed to help traders interpret chart screenshots, calculate precise risk parameters, and overcome emotional bias without replacing human agency.

Strategic Opportunity: Moving away from "black-box automated trade signals" to a transparent AI co-pilot that reinforces discipline and risk management.

Core User Journey & Scope

1. UploadChart Image
2. AI VisionPattern Recog.
3. SetupBias & SL/TP
4. Risk CheckGuardrail 1:2
5. DecisionHuman Gate
6. JournalAuto-Log
  • MVP Features: Multimodal vision analysis, structured Bias/Entry/SL/TP outputs, 1:2 R/R guardrail alerts, and one-click journaling.
  • Deliberately Out of Scope (V1): Direct broker execution and high-frequency trading (to prioritize risk safety and avoid compliance liability).
  • North Star Metric: Completed Analysis → Outcome Loop Rate (% of uploaded charts where users validate setups and log outcomes).
  • Quality Dimensions: Accuracy, Consistency, Relevance, Safety, and User Value.
Responsible AI Governance

02 / AI Loan Eligibility Assistant

A financial governance concept focused on building automated credit decisioning with built-in fairness, privacy, and explainability.

1. Bias Mitigation

Feature-masking applied to demographic attributes to prevent discriminatory scoring.

2. Privacy Protection

Zero data retention on sensitive personal financial records.

3. Explainability

Plain-language rationale generated for every automated recommendation audit log.

4. HITL Governance

Mandatory licensed loan officer review and sign-off required for all rejections.
